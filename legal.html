Last updated: April 30, 2026

We also collect standard web analytics data through Google Tag Manager, including pages visited, time on site, and referring URLs. This data is aggregated and not linked to individual identities.

We use the information you provide to:

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to deliver the service and maintain a business relationship. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. California residents have additional rights under CCPA, including the right to know what data we collect and to opt out of any sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information). To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@sentopi.com.

Sentopi provides review analysis reports for e-commerce products. We collect publicly available customer review data, analyze it, and deliver a prioritized action plan to the email address you provide.

The free report covers up to 100 reviews and delivers a summary of top complaint drivers and estimated revenue impact. Paid plans provide a full analysis with specific actions, competitor benchmarks, and month-over-month trend tracking.
