Common Questions

What Amazon sellers
ask before starting.

What does Sentopi do?
Sentopi runs the Retail Flywheel for your brand. Five levers drive an Amazon business: operations, pricing, assortment, visibility, and ratings. We score all five every week from your own listing data, name the one that is leaking, and go deep on it. When that lever is ratings, going deep means reading every review and mapping each complaint to a root cause. You get a ranked to-do list: what to fix first, who owns it, and what it is worth in annual revenue.
How is Sentopi different from Helium 10 or Jungle Scout?
Those tools hand you dashboards and leave the interpreting to you. Sentopi scores the five levers, names the weakest one, ties it to a dollar impact, and ranks the fixes. When the weak lever is ratings, we read every review rather than sampling sentiment. You leave with an action plan instead of another dashboard to operate.
Is it really free?
The five-lever read on any ASIN is free and instant, with no email required. Your first full product report is free too: paste your Amazon link and we score every lever and read 100 of your reviews, delivered within 48 hours with no credit card. The weekly report is $149 per month per product.
What do I need to give you to get started?
Just your Amazon product URL. Sentopi collects the full written review set automatically, so there is nothing to export and no Seller Central login required. Every source we use is public Amazon data.
How fast do I get results, and how accurate are they?
The free report lands within 48 hours. Findings are built from your actual reviews and public Amazon data. Each issue is scored by volume, severity, and fixability, so the ranking reflects real impact rather than how loud a single complaint is.
