Why Are My Amazon Reviews Not Showing Up? Six Causes, in Order

A customer told you they left a review; the listing says otherwise. Before you assume Amazon deleted it, two checks separate a delay from a filter from a removal, in about two minutes.

By Oliver Silen. Founder of Sentopi, former Amazon Vendor Manager and Product Manager. Published July 2026.

The short version

Most missing reviews trace to six causes: the normal 48-to-72-hour moderation window, the gap between ratings and written reviews, a policy filter, a low-history reviewer account, a variation merge or split that moved the review to a sibling ASIN, or a suppressed listing hiding the whole page. Start with two checks: the reviewer's public profile, and whether your ratings count moved without new text. Those two separate a delay from a filter from a removal before you escalate anything.

Missing reviews create a specific kind of seller anxiety, because each one feels like stolen social proof. Most of the time, nothing was stolen. The review is in a queue, or it arrived as a text-free rating, or it's sitting on a sibling ASIN after a variation change. The genuinely bad case, a policy removal, has a distinct signature you can verify in one click. Here's how to tell them apart without filing a pointless case with Seller Support.

I ran browse and search setup at Amazon as a Vendor Manager and Product Manager, close enough to the catalog machinery to know how much of what sellers call "Amazon deleting my reviews" is really the catalog doing what it always does: moderation queues, variation plumbing, and filters that were built to keep fake reviews out and occasionally catch real ones. The diagnostic below follows the same order I'd use on my own listing.

Two checks before anything else

Both take about a minute. The first: has your global ratings count moved without a new written review appearing? The second: is the review visible on the buyer's public profile? (Buyers can share their profile link; if you have the reviewer's Amazon profile, their reviews are listed there.) The combination tells you which branch you're on.

The six causes, in order of likelihood

Work down this list. Each entry names what to check and whether there's anything to do.

  1. Timing · no action needed The review is inside the moderation window. Every review passes through screening before it posts: typically 48 to 72 hours, stretching to two weeks for reviews with photos or video, reviews submitted during peak periods, and reviews that trip extra scrutiny (ZonGuru, 2026). Amazon also re-scans recently posted reviews, which is why a review can appear and then vanish days later. Check: when the review was submitted. Under three days old means wait; diagnosing now wastes the effort.
  2. Counting · 1 minute It was a rating, and there's no review to find. Since 2019, buyers can leave a star rating with one tap and no text, and verified-purchase ratings count toward the displayed star score (Perez, 2019). That's why the ratings number at the top of your listing runs far ahead of the written reviews below it; a shopper can leave many ratings in the time one written review takes (Sipp-Paris, 2025). Check: your global ratings count against your written review count, before and after. If the count moved with no new text, the feedback already landed.
  3. Policy · usually unrecoverable A policy filter caught it. Amazon removes reviews it classifies as incentivized, compensated, or written by someone connected to the seller (family, friends, employees), plus reviews with links, profanity, or seller-feedback content posted as a product review (ZonGuru, 2026). The filters read purchase history, account linkages, and language patterns, and they run retroactively. Check: whether the review is gone from the buyer's profile too. If it is, treat it as final: reinstatement requests almost never succeed, and coaching the buyer to repost risks your account, since the same filter fires again with your fingerprints on it.
  4. Reviewer account · outside your control The reviewer's account carries little weight. Amazon requires a minimum spend of $50 on the site in the past 12 months before an account can write reviews, and new or thin accounts get held or filtered more aggressively (TraceFuse, 2026). A burst of reviews from young accounts also triggers velocity screening across the whole listing. Check: nothing to fix on your side. If several reviews from a giveaway or launch batch vanished together, the account-weighting filter is your likely answer, and that batch is gone.
  5. Catalog · 5 minutes A variation merge or split moved them. Reviews display aggregated at the parent level, and each review stays attached to the child ASIN it was written on. Merge two families and the counts combine; split one, or detach a child, and reviews walk away with their ASIN (ZonGuru, 2026). Reviews also stay in the marketplace where they were written; a review on amazon.co.uk never shows on amazon.com. Check: sibling ASINs in the family, recent parentage changes in Manage Inventory, and which marketplace the buyer actually purchased in.
  6. Listing status · fixable The listing itself is suppressed. A suppressed or inactive listing hides its whole detail page, reviews included. Sellers hunting for missing reviews sometimes miss that the page they're staring at is a cached or direct-link version of a listing shoppers can't reach. Check: Fix Your Products in Seller Central for the suppression reason. Clear it and the reviews return with the page, since they were hidden rather than removed.
On recovery, be honest with yourself about the split: suppressed listings and variation moves give the reviews back; policy removals and filtered batches don't. The productive channel for volume you control is Amazon's own tooling, the Request a Review button and Vine, which generate verified feedback the filters leave alone.

The reviews you stop seeing are the ones that cost you

There's a second version of this problem that never gets a support case: reviews that display fine and still go unread, because they sit behind the default sort, on a sibling variation, or on page nine. Sellers read the first page and the one-star cluster after a bad week, and that sample misses the slow-building complaint patterns that drag a rating from 4.4 to 4.0 over a quarter. The stakes are asymmetric, since negative word of mouth moves sales more than positive (Chevalier & Mayzlin, 2006). Common questions

How long does it take for an Amazon review to show up?

Most reviews post within 48 to 72 hours. Reviews with photos or video, reviews submitted during peak periods, and reviews that trip extra screening can take up to two weeks. If the review was submitted less than three days ago, the most likely explanation is that it's still in the moderation queue, and the right move is to wait.

Why is my ratings count higher than my number of written reviews?

Since 2019, Amazon has let buyers leave a star rating without writing anything, and verified-purchase ratings count toward the displayed star score. The number at the top of your listing counts every rating; the review list below shows only the subset of buyers who wrote text. A large gap between the two is normal on every listing, and a rating that arrives with no review is still moving your average.

Can I get a removed Amazon review reinstated?

Almost never. Reviews removed for policy reasons, including incentivized reviews, reviews from people connected to the seller, and content violations, stay down, and asking the buyer to repost usually trips the same filter. Your time is better spent generating new verified reviews through Amazon's own channels, like the Request a Review button and the Vine program.

Where did my reviews go after I changed my variations?

Reviews display at the parent level, aggregated across child ASINs, and each review stays attached to the child ASIN it was written on. When a variation family is merged, split, or a child is detached, reviews follow their ASIN, so they can vanish from one page and surface on another. Check the other children in the family and your recent parentage changes in Manage Inventory before concluding anything was deleted.

Do reviews still show if my listing is suppressed?

No. A suppressed or inactive listing hides the whole detail page, reviews included. Check Fix Your Products in Seller Central for the suppression reason, resolve it, and the reviews come back with the page. Suppression hides reviews and removal deletes them, so confirm which one you're dealing with before you escalate.
